Delivery Methodology

A governance-first model for dependable Salesforce outcomes.

RPCS applies a transparent operating rhythm that gives business and technical stakeholders visibility into progress, risk, and value delivery at every milestone.

Guiding principles

  • Outcome-driven planning
  • Configuration-first architecture
  • Frequent stakeholder validation
  • Operational handoff readiness

Four-phase engagement model

Phase 1

Discover

Map goals, constraints, and value drivers. Document baseline process and data quality risk.

Phase 2

Design

Define architecture decisions, governance checkpoints, backlog sequencing, and release milestones.

Phase 3

Deliver

Execute in short iterations with build validation, UAT readiness, and measured release quality.

Phase 4

Optimize

Track adoption, stabilize operations, and run a data-backed enhancement roadmap after go-live.

Monthly operating rhythm

  • Monthly planning aligned to your most important business priorities
  • Focused execution updates with decisions, risks, and next steps kept clear
  • Targeted validation for changes affecting critical workflows
  • End-of-month summary with completed work, open items, and recommended priorities

What clients receive

  • Monthly summary of completed work, current priorities, and recommended next steps
  • Clear documentation for new configurations, automations, and process changes
  • Admin-friendly notes that make ongoing ownership easier
  • Practical guidance for future improvements as needs evolve
